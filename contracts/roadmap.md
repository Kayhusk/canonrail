# Roadmap contract

A roadmap records current sequence and decision authority.
Its presence does not authorize implementation.

## Owns

- The current phase and active decision gate.
- Ordered work that has been approved for planning or consideration.
- Explicitly deferred decisions and prerequisites.
- The authority required to move from proposal to implementation.
- The next decision, stated without implying that work has begun.

## Must not absorb

- Detailed implementation instructions better owned by an execution plan.
- Architecture copied from its durable owner.
- Completion claims without current evidence.
- Historical receipts that no longer affect the next decision.
- Blanket authority to install, deploy, publish, or change production systems.

## Review

- Separate proposed, approved, implemented, and verified states.
- Confirm that the next item follows current dependencies rather than visual order.
- Remove stale status and duplicated technical detail.
- State what the roadmap does not authorize when ambiguity could trigger action.
- Verify the active gate against the current project sources.
